# De-novo multi-heme protein junctions



* Online meeting (Jochen, Isma, Yongchan, ZF)
* Date: 14. 10. 2026, 2 pm



* Distance-decay project: Isma & Yongchan will send a manuscript, Jochen & ZF will add computational contributions (double-mutant STC, GSU, MtrC... junction structures, conductivities normalized to DOS and number of contacts)
* STC gating: compare the computational curves (potential drop and symmetry factor effects, fit to the experimental data, write the contribution to the manuscript)



* Calculations are done on mono-heme, di-heme, and tetra-heme de-novo structures
* Hemes were removed from the structures  (apo vs holo structures) => no change in the calculated current
* No effect of the heme on the tunneling conductance was confirmed by the experimental measurements
* The biggest limitation of the calculations is the limited sampling (3 structures only, selected by the number of contacts with the electrodes)
* The width of the conductance peak should increase after removing the heme (thermal fluctuation effect, the structures with hemes are more rigid) => this should be explored on tetraheme (data on di-heme are inconclusive)
* The gating measurements should be very sensitive to heme removal (the di-heme protein should be ideal for checking... if the gating effect is not detected, it would indicate that the hopping is not the dominant mechanism there... could be, as the de-novo structures were not optimized to maximize the heme-heme couplings)
* Other gating experiments could be done on de-nove tetra-heme (direct comparison with STC... hemes are closer to each other in STC)
